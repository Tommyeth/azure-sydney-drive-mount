Azure Sydney Drive Mount

A personal application that uses rclone to mount its owner's Google Drive folder as a directory on the owner's server.

The application supports listing, reading, creating, updating and deleting files as requested by its owner. It is intended for the owner's personal use, not a public file-sharing service.

Google Drive data stays in the owner's Google account and on devices or servers controlled by the owner, including local file caches needed for the mount.
