Privacy policy

Azure Sydney Drive Mount is a personal utility operated by its Google account owner. Last updated: 4 October 2026.

Data access and use

With the owner's authorization, rclone accesses Google Drive file metadata and file contents to provide a readable and writable directory. It can list, read, create, update and delete files. Its Google Drive OAuth permission can cover the entire Drive; the configured mount exposes the folder selected by the owner.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and file caches are stored on the owner's devices and server. Requests are sent to Google's services to perform the requested operations. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or provide it to unrelated third parties.

Retention and control

The owner controls cached files and credentials and can remove them from their devices, stop the mount, or revoke the app's access in Google Account permissions. Cloud files remain in Google Drive unless the owner deletes them. Deleting a local cache alone does not delete the cloud copy.

Limited use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

The application's support contact is the owner email shown on its Google OAuth consent screen.
